Is my starter ready to use?
  • It has at least doubled in size since feeding
  • The surface is domed, not flat or starting to collapse
  • It smells tangy and yeasty, not sour or alcoholic
  • A small spoonful dropped in water floats (the classic float test)
  • You can see plenty of bubbles on the sides of the jar

Timing genuinely matters. Use your starter at or just after peak activity. Too early gives you a slow, reluctant rise. Too late gives you an over-fermented, flat loaf. Both are recoverable, but why make life harder?
